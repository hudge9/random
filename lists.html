Dictionary Words

Definition List

Monomyth
(n.)
A Hero's Journey consisting of stages such as the call to adventure, crossing the threshold, and return.

Hero's Journey
(n.)
A journey undertaken by a hero in myths and stories.

Archetype
(n.)
A typical character, an action, or a situation that seems to represent universal patterns of human nature.

Villian
(n.)
An archetype that opposes the hero and creates conflict in the story.

Hero
(n.)
The main character in a story, often displaying courage and the willingness to sacrifice for others.
